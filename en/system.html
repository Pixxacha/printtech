Our System

PRINTTECH LED UV system for commercial sheetfed offset and narrow web presses.

System Configurations

Sheetfed offset or narrow web presses

Can be fitted to an existing press (currently running oil-based inks and moving to UV-curing inks) or to a new press.

Touchscreen control

Two touchscreens: one at the driver unit and one at the colour control desk. The software supports Thai, and operators can easily set how the LED UV lamps switch on to suit their work.

LED lamp optics

Designed so the LED UV lamp unit can be mounted 30–110 mm from the gripper while keeping high light intensity, so prints cure fully even at the press's top speed.

High light intensity

Available in two wavelengths, 385 nm and 395 nm. The system can also be tuned to the type of work a printer runs, such as books, paper packaging, plastic or metal.

LED lamp housing

Made of metal alloy and compact, it fits sheetfed offset and narrow web presses of any make, Japanese or European.

Cooling system

Water is used as the cooling medium, which keeps the UV light output even.
