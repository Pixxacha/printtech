What is an LED UV system?

PRINTTECH LED UV SYSTEM

Printing with LED UV curing uses more than three times less electricity than conventional UV systems (mercury lamps), which are the common standard today.

While printing, no heat is produced that could damage the substrate, and no ozone is created, so it is safe for press operators and kind to the environment.

Smooth, trouble-free printing

With LED UV, prints dry instantly. This cuts production time at the printing stage by at least 30% compared with oil-based inks, because there is no waiting for sheets to dry before printing the back, and it reduces losses from smudged backs and reprints.

It also needs no spray powder, so there is no powder dust on the printed surface. This suits high-quality work such as premium catalogues (cars, jewellery, cosmetics).
